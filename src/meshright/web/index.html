        Report a problem

        MeshRight puts together what we need to look into it. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your email app.

        What goes in the report

        • The last error messages MeshRight showed
        • The steps you took on this model (the History list)
        • MeshRight version, browser and operating system
        • The model's name, size and triangle count, not the model itself

        Steam Vibe Ltd is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office, ICO Reg. ZC095648. If you include your model, it is used only to reproduce the fault. It is never shared, distributed or sold, and it is deleted once the fault is fixed.

            Best way to print

            Trying different ways to place it…

            Support amounts are estimates for comparing options; your slicer makes the real supports.

            Make a bust

            For a scan of a head, or head and shoulders. Closes the opening at the neck, cuts the ragged bottom off flat, adds a base and sizes it.

            Stand the head upright first. If it is lying down, use Which way is up?

            Make a part

            Turns the blue area into a solid piece shaped to fit it, such as a pad, a grip or a cover. It opens as a new model; this one stays as it is.

            3MF in colours

            Groups the scan's colours into a few print colours and paints every triangle, so Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer and OrcaSlicer open it ready for a multi-colour printer. In the slicer, give each colour a filament.

            Fewer colours give cleaner areas. Four suits most scans; use as many as your printer has filaments.

            Hollow for resin

            Makes the inside hollow to save resin, keeping walls of this thickness, with drain holes at the bottom so trapped resin can run out.

            Your printer uses filament: the slicer's infill already fills the inside sparsely, so you usually do not need this.

            Ask AI

            Ask in your own words, for example "will this print?" or "make it 15 cm tall and fix it". The AI sees the check report, never your model. It can only suggest changes; nothing happens until you press Apply, and each step can be undone.

              Give it thickness

              Turns an open surface, such as a face scan, mask or relief, into a solid shell. The scanned surface stays as it is; the thickness goes behind it.

              2 mm suits most filament prints. Use at least twice your nozzle size.
