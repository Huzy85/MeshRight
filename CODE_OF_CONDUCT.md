# Code of conduct

MeshRight is for everyone, from first-time printers to experts. Be kind, be
patient with beginners, and assume good intent.

- Explain things plainly; nobody is expected to know the jargon.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Do not share other people's personal data, or models you do not have the
  right to share.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Maintainers may remove comments or contributions that break these rules.
Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub.
